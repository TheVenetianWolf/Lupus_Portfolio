The hunt is the job title

Aug 2026 · community

Why I started the Wild Hunt Project instead of waiting for a nicer label on LinkedIn.

Job titles lag behind the work. I was already hunting broken things — in labs, in CTFs, in other people's production — long before anyone paid me to. The Wild Hunt Project is that instinct written down: a public rule-set for people who refuse to walk past a problem.

Recruiters ask what I "am". The honest answer is narrower than a stack list and wider than intern. I study computer science at Trinity, specialising in AI and security. I ship simulations, I disclose bugs, I run a pack. The through-line is the hunt.

// note 002

Fenrir, or making pursuit visible

2026 · engineering

A Sunday curiosity that became a public Python lab for missile guidance and pursuit.

Fenrir started as caffeine and a question: can you see proportional navigation, or does it only live in lecture slides? The dashboard is the answer. Live trajectories, evasive targets, telemetry, a radar view — Python and Streamlit, all of it runnable.

I published it because a simulation you cannot clone is just a screenshot. Repo: TheVenetianWolf/Fenrir.
